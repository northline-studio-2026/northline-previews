What we clean

Every job starts with an inspection, so the clean is matched to your carpet, rug or fabric.

Carpet Cleaning

Deep hot-water steam extraction flushes out trapped grit, dust and allergens from the base of the carpet. Touch-dry in 2–4 hours.

Upholstery Cleaning

Sofas, armchairs and suites deep cleaned, with mites and pollutants removed. Car interiors cleaned too.

Rug Cleaning

Gentle specialist care for delicate wool carpets, Persian rugs and oriental area rugs.

Stain Removal

Stubborn stains inspected first, then treated with the right method for your fabric — no guesswork.

Commercial Cleaning

Offices, retail premises, medical clinics and schools. Weekend and out-of-hours work so your team walks in Monday to fresh carpets.

Mattress Cleaning

Mattresses deep cleaned and refreshed, with dust and allergens lifted out for a healthier night’s sleep.

Also covered: drapery and curtain cleaning, floor cleaning, and water damage restoration.
