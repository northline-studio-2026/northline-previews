What we do

Stuck? We fix it
where you stand.

Every service below is one we actually run, day and night, across the Las Vegas valley. No booking system, no waiting for a callback — call and a truck rolls.

01

Flatbed Towing

Damage-free transport for cars, trucks, RVs and luxury vehicles. Flatbed is the preferred method — no wheel drag, no transmission stress.

02

Jump Starts & Battery

Heavy-duty equipment revives a dead battery on the spot — or a new battery installed right there, with diagnostics to find the real fault.

03

Flat Tire Service

On-site repair, patching or a spare swap — handled properly, not a roadside bodge, so you can drive on with confidence.

04

Lockout Assistance

Keys locked inside? Non-damaging tools get you back in without scratches, dents or a broken window.

05

Fuel Delivery

Ran dry on the way to Hoover Dam or the Strip? A quick fuel drop gets you to the nearest station — no long walk in the desert heat.

06

Winching & Recovery

Pulled off the road, stuck in sand or in a ditch? Safe winching and recovery gets your vehicle out without further damage.

Also covered: long-distance towing (including Las Vegas to Kingman, AZ), accident & heavy-duty towing, wheel-lift service, junk car removal and cash for cars.

How it works

Three steps.
Then you’re moving.

There is no online booking and no account to create. The whole job starts with one phone call — that is the point.

1

Call or text

Call (702) 603-6623 or text (702) 516-7681 — any hour, any day. A real local dispatcher answers, not a phone tree.

2

Tell us where & what

Your location, your vehicle, and what happened. You get a straight quote and an honest ETA before anything moves — typically 30–60 minutes in the valley.

3

Truck arrives, problem solved

The crew fixes it on the spot or tows you to the shop, dealer or home of your choice. You are back on the road — and you only pay for the help you needed.
