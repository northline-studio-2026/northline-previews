What we do

From a full new Colorbond roof to a single leaking valley — every job starts with a free assessment and an honest quote.

🏠

New Colorbond Roofing

New roofs and extensions in Colorbond, cement and terracotta tile — matched to your existing roof so the extension looks like it was always there.

🔧

Roof Repairs

Broken tiles replaced with closely matched tiles, rusty valley irons swapped out, lead flashings and damaged battens repaired — done right, first time.

🌧️

Colorbond Guttering & Fascia

Old, rusted gutters causing leaks under the eaves, replaced with Ace Gutters in a range of Colorbond colours — guaranteed for 20 years.

💦

High-Pressure Roof Cleaning

Full top-to-bottom clean: gutters and downpipes washed, capping re-bedded and re-pointed, sealer applied and three coats of colour. Your roof looks new again.

✨

Roof Restoration

When a roof is damaged beyond a simple repair, we bring it back from the brink at a fraction of replacement cost — pressure cleaned, rust-treated and resprayed.

💧

Roof Leak Repairs

Leaks at corners, moisture patches on the ceiling, water getting in after heavy rain — we find the cause and fix it properly, not just patch over it.

Also covered: Whirlybird installation (wind-driven and electric) to keep your home cooler and cut energy bills.
