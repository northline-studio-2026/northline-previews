What this rebuild fixes

Four small trust problems on the live store — each one costs a sale that the ads already paid for.

01

Prove the rating, or drop it

The homepage badge claims “Rated 4.7/5” with no source and no link. A claim nobody can check reads as a claim nobody should trust. The rebuild shows real reviews with their source — or removes the badge entirely.

02

Reviews that match the ad spend

Product pages show 2–5 reviews each while 27 ads drive traffic to them. Shoppers compare the two and walk away. The rebuild surfaces every genuine review on the product page itself.

03

Urgency with a real end date

“Offer ends in days” — with no end date, the countdown is always on and never believed. The rebuild gives every offer a real date, or removes the countdown.

04

Clean names, one shipping message

The free-shipping strip repeats 9 times on the page, and category tiles are labelled with raw filenames like #color_clay. The rebuild says it once, and names every category in plain English.
