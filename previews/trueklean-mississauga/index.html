What we do

Carpets, tile & grout —
restored, not just rinsed.

Every job uses the same double-clean method: a CRB counter-rotating machine with encapsulation, followed by hot-water extraction for the deep stuff.

Steam Carpet Cleaning

Hot-water extraction that reaches deep into carpet fibres to lift stubborn dirt and allergens — and sanitises as it cleans.

Encapsulation Cleaning

A low-moisture method that traps dirt in crystals for a deep clean with quick drying — ideal for high-traffic homes.

Tile & Grout Cleaning

Specialised brushes and sealers restore shine to porcelain tiles and grout lines in kitchens and bathrooms.
